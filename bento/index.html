Since 1951

0 Years of Engineering Leadership

Founded on 12 June 1951 as an IRE professional group, TEMS has grown with the profession it serves.

  1. 1951IRE Professional Group on Engineering Management
  2. 1973IEEE Engineering Management Society
  3. 2007IEEE Technology Management Council
  4. 2015IEEE Technology and Engineering Management Society

What we focus on

Five Focus Areas

  • Moving products and services from idea to market
  • Identifying and implementing successful projects and systems
  • Integrating technology for capability and productivity
  • Developing from engineer to leader
  • Balancing the norms of society, government and regulators
